The idea Reel 1

a film club in Dubai, for people who stay.

In most cinemas, the lights come up and everyone leaves. We think that's where the best part begins. Post Credits is a moment built around a film and the people watching it: a classic on a big screen, then a real conversation, then the night that follows. A place to discover films, to share the ones that shaped you, and to meet people who care about them as much as you do.

Film

One film a night. The classics you've heard of and never seen on a big screen, revealed 48 hours before.

Talk

Nobody leaves when the credits roll. Small tables, one question, no lecture. Bring an opinion, leave with a new one.

After

The bar, the people, the argument you didn't finish. Everyone brings the films that speak to them.
